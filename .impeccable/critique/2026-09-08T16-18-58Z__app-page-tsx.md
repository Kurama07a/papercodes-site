## Design Specificity Verdict

The typography, black/paper/red palette, systems language, hard edges, and editorial indexing belong to PaperCodes. The composition remains only partially authored because it separates paper, grid, imagery, handwriting, and technical logic into clean modules. The reference makes those media collide on one plane.

The deterministic detector found 25 advisories in `app/globals.css`: 19 type-scale mismatches and 6 undocumented neutral colors. Most palette findings and several display-size findings are documentation-level false positives. The accepted micro sizes still reinforce a genuine readability concern.

No user-visible detector overlay was available because no mutable browser surface was exposed. Independent headless Chromium checks at 1440px, 390px, and 320px found no horizontal clipping and confirmed sensible semantic ordering.

## Overall Impression

The opening proposition is confident and the information architecture is coherent. The single biggest opportunity is to make physical rupture the organizing grid rather than a future asset layer. The current result explains PaperCodes clearly but does not yet feel raw, restless, human, or visually inevitable.

## What's Working

- Instrument Serif, technical mono, warm paper, black engine, and restrained signal red create a coherent base voice.
- The proposition and surface-versus-system story are understood immediately.
- The workflow and capability content make the offer concrete.

## Priority Issues

### P1: The torn reveal is missing as an organizing structure

The reference is not a white column beside a black rectangle. Paper tears diagonally into the engine while photography, diagrams, marks, and grid occupy the same plane. Rebuild the hero and ethos around reusable irregular masks and overlapping layers before importing final assets.

Suggested command: `$impeccable overdrive`

### P1: The page is a modular specification rather than an interlocked dossier

The reference compresses several ideas into dense spreads. The implementation gives nearly every idea a separate rectangular band and is roughly 2.26 times taller at equal width. Consolidate the narrative into three or four visual acts and replace the equal process boxes with a visible returning path.

Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

### P1: The middle lacks material and emotional contrast

The reference moves from rupture to chaos to proof to human conviction. The implementation moves from proposition to diagram to taxonomy to another diagram. Use a few decisive assets: hero rupture, documentary chaos collage, real project/interface plates, and architectural ethos photography.

Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder`

### P1: Evidence does not earn its claim

Exact synthetic metrics dominate despite every card being labeled CONCEPT. Rename the section to Concept logs or System prototypes until verified work exists, and prefer inspectable artifacts or working demonstrations over invented outcome numbers.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

### P1: Supporting typography is too small and too uniformly monospaced

Many explanatory passages sit around 10 to 11px, with some closer to 9px. Reserve mono for labels, IDs, diagrams, and metadata. Use a readable prose treatment around 14 to 16px, with 11 to 12px reserved for true microcopy.

Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

### P2: Static elements imply interactions that do not exist

Architecture plus signs look expandable, the play icon implies video, and Form submitted looks like a control. Either implement those behaviors or make the elements unmistakably diagrammatic.

Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, first-time buyer:** understands the proposition, then encounters unexplained system terminology and a contradiction between Evidence and illustrative concepts.

**Riley, skeptical operator:** will test plus marks and status-like controls, then question synthetic metrics and staged online language.

**Casey, mobile visitor:** faces more than twelve common phone viewports, very small mono text, and few conversion moments through the long middle.

## Minor Observations

- The desktop hero engine leaves its lower half empty where the reference uses torn transitions, photography, diagrams, and annotation.
- Same spirit, Different form uses italic serif rather than a convincingly handwritten voice.
- The tool band is credible, though the final logo becomes an orphan in the two-column mobile grid.
- Capabilities is one of the stronger static sections and should remain an index.
- Mobile ordering is sensible and avoids horizontal overflow.

## Questions to Consider

- If generated imagery disappeared, what one silhouette would still make the homepage unmistakably PaperCodes?
- Does Evidence earn its place while every result is illustrative?
- Could the required content live in three or four interlocked editorial acts rather than nine separate bands?
- Should static diagrams look operational, or explicitly diagrammatic until they can respond?
- Is the intended feeling before conversion relief, rebellion, confidence, or curiosity?
