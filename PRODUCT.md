# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, semantic HTML, custom CSS, SVG for diagrams, and GSAP only where later motion work requires it. This stack is specified in `Prompt.md`.

## Users

The primary audience is owners and operators of growing businesses whose customer journey and internal operations are fragmented across websites, forms, inboxes, spreadsheets, CRMs, SaaS tools, APIs, databases, and AI models. They are evaluating whether PaperCodes can turn that operational mess into one coherent system.

## Product Purpose

PaperCodes is a digital systems agency that designs customer-facing experiences and engineers the systems behind them. The website should help qualified visitors understand the offer, see credible evidence of the approach, and start a project.

## Positioning

PaperCodes joins four layers that are usually sold separately: experience, data, automation, and intelligence. It turns messy business processes into working software rather than merely adding another disconnected tool.

## Operating Context

Typical work includes websites, landing pages, portals, CRM architecture, lead and customer systems, n8n and Make workflows, APIs, webhooks, AI agents, internal tools, dashboards, and custom software.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required routes: home, work index, project detail, services, approach, about, and start-project.
- The homepage must tell the surface-versus-engine story and make the four-layer architecture understandable.
- The start-project route must eventually provide an accessible, validated intake flow without pretending a backend exists.
- Project evidence and metrics must be editable structured data and identified as illustrative until real client evidence is supplied.
- Important typography and technical diagrams remain semantic HTML or SVG.
- The implementation must be responsive, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion aware, performant, and SEO-ready.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PaperCodes.
- Core promise: “PaperCodes turns messy processes into working software.”
- Primary proposition: “Design what they see. Automate what they don’t.”
- Voice: concise, confident, thoughtful, technical, creative, and slightly rebellious; never corporate or AI-hype-heavy.
- The identity must support an agency, technology consultancy, product studio, and future software products.
- The brand may feel raw, precise, restless, industrial, human, and experimental, but must not reproduce Linkin Park logos, artwork, photography, lyrics, typography, or recognizable iconography.

## Product Principles

1. Treat the visible experience and the operational engine as one designed system.
2. Replace repeated manual work with clear, connected, inspectable software.
3. Demonstrate concrete system behavior instead of relying on generic technology claims.
4. Adapt the solution to the business rather than forcing every client into one template.
5. Preserve human clarity while engineering technical depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website requires semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, meaningful image alternatives, accessible form errors, reduced-motion support, and equivalent non-drag controls for reveal interactions.
